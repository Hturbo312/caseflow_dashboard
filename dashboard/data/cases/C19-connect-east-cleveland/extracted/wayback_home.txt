Your vision.
Connect EC is an interactive process to reimagine and rebuild the public spaces in East Cleveland through community collaboration. Starting at the western gateway to the city, the process focused on recommendations around the Circle East development led by the Cuyahoga Land Bank.

East Cleveland.
Located in the southwestern corner of East Cleveland, the focus area for the project is adjacent to Lakeview Cemetery and Cleveland’s University Circle. Project concepts will encourage greater connectivity within the neighborhood and between the neighborhood and amenities such as Lakeview Cemetery, Windermere & Superior Stations, and University Circle, including enhanced multimodal access.
Focus area.
The placemaking plan for the focus area will concentrate on the Euclid Avenue commercial corridor and the adjacent residential areas.  The community process will explore streetscape elements, a vision for the greenspaces in the neighborhood, and a potential entryway to Lakeview Cemetery at the western end of Forest Hill Avenue.

Project priorities.
Build a physical environment that promotes community cohesion, trust, and safety.
Improve accessibility to increase connectivity and walkability.
Create signage and public art to set this neighborhood apart as a distinct place.
Provide amenities for neighborhood green spaces that are inviting and useful.
The process will complement the residential development planned for the
Circle East
district. Together, we will create the physical, cultural, and social identities that will define this neighborhood and support its ongoing evolution.
