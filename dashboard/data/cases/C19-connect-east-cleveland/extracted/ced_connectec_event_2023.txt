Circle East District and ConnectEC Invite Community to Reimagine the City
By
CED_Michael
August 23, 2023
May 9th, 2024
No Comments
1 min read
The City of East Cleveland, in partnership with the Cuyahoga Land Bank, continues to invite community members for collaborative efforts to reimagine and rebuild spaces in the city. Circle East District is a Land Bank initiative created to help revitalize the residential and commercial areas at East Cleveland’s gateway to University Circle.
