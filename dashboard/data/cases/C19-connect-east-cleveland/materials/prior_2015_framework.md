Markdown Content:
## What happened?

Cloudflare cannot validate the SSL certificate on the origin web server. The certificate may be expired, self-signed, or may not cover the requested domain name.

## What can I do?

### If you're a visitor of this website:

Please try again in a few minutes.

### If you're the owner of this website:

Please refer to the [Error 526](https://developers.cloudflare.com/support/troubleshooting/http-status-codes/cloudflare-5xx-errors/error-526/) article:

*   Make sure the certificate you are using is not expired and fully covers the requested domain name.
*   Check [Origin Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/support/troubleshooting/http-status-codes/cloudflare-5xx-errors/error-526/#diagnose-with-origin-analytics).
*   Install a free [Cloudflare Origin CA certificate](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ssl/origin-configuration/origin-ca/) on your origin server.
*   Contact your hosting provider to update or replace the SSL certificate on the origin server.
