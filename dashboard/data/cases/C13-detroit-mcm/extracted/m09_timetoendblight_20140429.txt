Our Mission:
To develop a straightforward and detailed implementation plan to remove every 'blighted' residential structure, commercial structure and public building, and clear every blighted vacant lot in the city of Detroit as quickly as possible using an environmentally-conscious approach. The plan's recommendations will focus on creating economic opportunities for the city and its people, as well as dramatically improving the safety of residents and first responders.
