Last week, children and teenagers of Prishtina were actively engaged in co-designing child-friendly public spaces using [UrbanistAI](https://it.linkedin.com/company/urbanistai?trk=public_post-text). This initiative was made possible through a collaboration with [UNDP Kosovo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/undp-kosovo?trk=public_post-text) and the Municipality of Prishtina, through the [#CityExperimentFund](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fcityexperimentfund&trk=public_post-text) (CEF). Pupils from various schools in Prishtina utilized the platform to collaboratively create safe, environmentally conscious, and socially inclusive spaces for children aged 10 and above. The workshop aimed to tap into the collective intelligence of the participants, maximizing community values through participatory planning. Children had the opportunity to generate and visualize their ideas on how an ideal area for them to spend time should look like using the [UrbanistAI](https://it.linkedin.com/company/urbanistai?trk=public_post-text) platform. The tool facilitated the expression of their needs while enabling collaborative decision-making. Soon, the locations of [#Prishtina](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fprishtina&trk=public_post-text) where children are co-designing will take a new look. Stay connected to witness these changes unfold! This event was supported by the UNDP Kosovo City Experiment Fund.
