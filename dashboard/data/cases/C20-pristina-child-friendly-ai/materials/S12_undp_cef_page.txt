## Shaping urban futures

![Image 2](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF-Logo_color-2-scaled.png)

The City Experiment Fund (CEF) is a UNDP-led initiative that collaborates with cities across Europe and Central Asia to tackle complex urban challenges through strategic innovation and experimentation. CEF provides technical and financial support to develop innovation portfolios that test new approaches and drive sustainable urban transformation.

By applying systems thinking, we help cities explore, reimagine, and finance more resilient, inclusive, and sustainable urban futures – and co-create practical, scalable solutions that address development challenges.

[Our current CEF cohort focuses on the Twin Transition](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/undp-announces-new-cohort-of-cities-for-the-city-experiment-fund/) – integrating digital and green innovations to transform urban infrastructure, governance, and services, ensuring cities are future-ready and adaptive.

_CEF is made possible with the financial support of the Ministry of Finance of the Slovak Republic._

## Our programmes

*   The 2025 Twin Transition Cohort brings together 10 cities across Europe and Central Asia to explore how green and digital transformation can reinforce one another. Some cities are joining CEF for the first time, while others are building on the experience and lessons gained in previous cohorts. Together, they design and test portfolios of interventions, exchange knowledge, and develop new approaches to urban transformation. 
*   CEF x AI extends the portfolio approach to help cities adopt, govern, and benefit from artificial intelligence (AI) in ways that are practical, inclusive, and aligned with the public interest. Through two complementary tracks – a demonstrator track focused on systemic, portfolio-based AI transformation and a BOOST track that matches cities with innovators to co-design GenAI solutions – the programme will support selected cities in exploring where AI can create public value and in building the capabilities needed to shape and govern these technologies. 

## What's the portfolio approach?

CEF uses systems thinking to design portfolios for urban transformation. UNDP works with cities and partners to create portfolios of solutions by exploring problems from multiple perspectives, identifying opportunities for change, designing a range of interconnected interventions aligned with a shared vision, and continuously adapting and managing the portfolio as new insights emerge. Rather than relying on isolated, one-off projects, this approach combines complementary interventions that reinforce one another, evolve over time, and help address the root causes of complex urban challenges.

## Why portfolios?

### Holistic problem-solving

Tackles interrelated urban challenges from multiple angles, especially in times of uncertainty.

### Dynamic adaptation

Continuously refines solutions based on learning.

### Informed decision-making

Uses feedback loops and data-driven insights to improve urban policies.

## Meet our city cohorts

CEF works with cities over multiple years, helping them navigate emerging urban challenges. Our programmes bring together municipalities from across Europe and Central Asia to test new ideas, build capabilities, and shape more sustainable, resilient, and future-ready urban systems.

## Thematic focus areas

Urban challenges are interconnected and require comprehensive, systemic solutions. CEF focuses on five key areas that enable cities to transition toward greener, smarter, and more resilient urban systems. We help cities address sustainability, digitalization, and governance challenges in ways that are adaptive, inclusive, and scalable.

![Image 5](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-01.png)

### DRIVING SUSTAINABILITY

Supporting cities in their green transition, reducing emissions, and promoting low-carbon and environmentally friendly urban development.

![Image 6](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-02.png)

### ENABLING CIRCULAR ECONOMY

Transforming urban resource flows, business models, and consumption patterns to create more closed-loop and sustainable systems.

![Image 7](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-03.png)

### IMPROVING QUALITY OF LIFE

Enhancing air quality, access to green spaces, and sustainable mobility, ensuring urban areas are more livable, inclusive, and equitable.

![Image 8](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-04.png)

### RESHAPING URBAN SYSTEMS

Rethinking energy, transportation, waste management, and land use to build more resilient, efficient, and people-centered cities.

![Image 9](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-05.png)

### FOSTERING RESILIENCE

Strengthening cities’ ability to withstand climate shocks, water scarcity, and biodiversity loss through green infrastructure, renewable energy, and nature-based solutions.

## Timeline

CEF is a multi-year initiative that supports cities through successive phases of exploration, experimentation, learning and adapting, and scaling. Cities develop and test portfolios of green and digital interventions, refine them based on evidence and feedback, and integrate successful approaches and lessons learned into long-term policies and investment decisions.

![Image 11](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-06.png)

2025

Starting new city cohort focused on twin transition. Setting up strategic innovation portfolios with cities. Exploring innovative financing approaches.

![Image 12](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-07.png)

2026

Review and iterate city portfolios. Expanding and deepening CEF portfolios into new areas. Strengthening connections between BOOST and city projects. Exploring how AI can support urban transformation portfolios.

![Image 13](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-07.png)

2027

Testing innovative financing mechanisms for urban transformation. Scaling successful approaches. Testing practical AI applications in real municipal contexts.

![Image 14](https://innovation.eurasia.undp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CEF_webpage_elements_icons-07.png)

2028

Ensuring foundations for portfolios to continue independently. Sharing what we learned with other cities and partners. Creating guidelines for scaling urban transformation. Building a regional knowledge base on AI for cities.
