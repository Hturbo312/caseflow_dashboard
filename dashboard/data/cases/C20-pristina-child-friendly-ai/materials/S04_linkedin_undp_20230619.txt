💡💭 Reimagining outdoor spaces for socializing in Pristina with [#AI](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fai&trk=public_post-text)-powered simulations! Together with children and teenagers in Pristina we held a co-design session using UrbanistAI to design child-friendly public spaces - a platform that combines [#ArtificialIntelligence](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fartificialintelligence&trk=public_post-text) with [#UrbanDesign](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Furbandesign&trk=public_post-text). 👈 Using the platform, students from various schools in Pristina conceptualized different ideas to create safe, environmentally conscious, and socially inclusive spaces. 🧩 Combining child-friendly cities, collaborative decision-making and innovation, the activity was developed as an addendum to the co-design process so far identifying appropriate locations and functions for children aged 10 and older within public and green areas in the city. 🌳 In the near future, [#Pristina](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fpristina&trk=public_post-text)'s locations where children are co-designing will undergo a transformation. Stay tuned to see how these changes unfold! This initiative was made possible through a collaboration with the Komuna e Prishtinës. [Maria Suokko](https://fi.linkedin.com/in/maria-suokko-b426713?trk=public_post-text) - UNDP Resident Representative in Kosovo Embassy of the Grand-Duchy of Luxembourg in Kosovo [#cityexperimentfund](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fcityexperimentfund&trk=public_post-text) (CEF) Respect Our Rights - ROR [Enis Mustafa](https://ch.linkedin.com/in/enismustafa?trk=public_post-text) - Communication Office
