Coverage Goal: To bring free WiFi to the entire neighborhood, including
the NYCHA Red Hook Houses, by 2020.

 

Coverage

Over time Red Hook WiFi will expand to provide free internet service throughout the commercial corridors of the community, using 40+ access points and 14 resilient hotspots. 
Resilient Hotspots
 are sites equipped with alternative power sources to ensure continuity in the event of power outages.
