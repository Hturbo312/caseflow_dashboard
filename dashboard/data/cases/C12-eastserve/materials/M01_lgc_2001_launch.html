MINISTER LAUNCHES EASTSERVE.COM FOR EAST MANCHESTER RESIDENTS

08 March 2001 By LGC Contributor

A new interactive website giving East Manchester residents the chance to gain information on local job opportunitie...

A new interactive website giving East Manchester residents the chance to gain information on local job opportunities, respond to police requests for information, handle housing concerns and deal with a whole host of other services all at the touch of button, will be officially launched by Electronic Government Minister Ian McCartney on March 13.
Eastserve.com will give local people 24-hour electronic access via the Internet to wide range of community, voluntary and commercial information. It will also provide interactive public service in the four prioritised areas of housing, employment, environment and police ? all specific to their area.
Eastserve.com will be accessible through both PCs and TV, with content being automatically restyled according to the device.
The project has been developed through the New Deal for Communities Initiative, in partnership with the private sector led by local company ClicksandLinks Ltd.
The programme gives 450 people in the Beswick, Openshaw and Clayton areas of the city the chance to take part in the pilot project by providing them with either a low cost computer or a free set-top box for use with their television together with Internet access at reduced rates.
The local community has played an integral role in the development of Eastserve.com. It has been their demands and priorities that have contributed to the contents of the website and how it is used. As well as providing technology within people’s homes, the project will also provide access to the Internet at selected community locations within the area.
Councillor Kath Robinson, Deputy Leader of Manchester City Council commented: ‘The development of this project will bring some of the latest advances in technology to some of the most deprived homes in the city.
It will give ordinary people access to useful local information, providing them with a chance to learn ICT skills that may be useful for future training or job opportunities. It will also give them a chance to communicate quickly and efficiently with the City Council and other agencies. ‘
One of the key aims of Eastserve.com is to improve service delivery to local residents by encouraging interactive use of the website. Among the many uses of the new website will be the chance for users to view their own rent accounts, book appointments with housing officers, report faults to their home, communicate with the Police and deal with problems of street lighting and cleansing.
It will provide users with templates for CV’s and job applications as well as a comprehensive database of local jobs opportunities.
Cllr Robinson continued: ‘This is an exciting project which has proved extremely popular with local people and the community. It has been community focussed from the start and our consultation with the local community will continue particularly in the first six months of the project when the website may need refining. If the project is successful in East Manchester, it will be rolled out across the city.’
Eastserve.com is part of wider ICT strategy in East Manchester, which includes current bids for Wired up Community and UK Online Centre funding.
