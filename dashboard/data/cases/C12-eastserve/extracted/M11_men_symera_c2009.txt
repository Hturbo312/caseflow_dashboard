A COMMUNITY broadband network is bringing the digital world to residents in east Manchester to ensure they stay connected.
‌
‌
Beverley Hughes MP, Minister for the North West visited the area this week to witness New East Manchester and Manchester City Council sign up internet service provider, Symera Technologies, as a private-sector partner to run Eastserve.
‌
Eastserve is the largest community wireless broadband network in the UK and will allow the award-winning internet provider to expand its network beyond east Manchester and enable more people without a landline to access an affordable wireless connection.
The service was set up in 2000 to tackle the digital divide in east Manchester and to increase the number of residents owning a computer and accessing the internet as well as improving their IT skills.
More than 5,500 local residents have received a subsidised computer and been on computer training courses. Eastserve has more than 1,500 paying customers signed up to its wireless broadband service and also offers an affordable PC repair centre for users and non-users.
‌
Sean McGonigle, director of regeneration for New East Manchester, said: "When Eastserve was first set up in 2000 it was truly innovative and when the wireless broadband network was launched in 2003 it became the biggest community broadband network in Europe. It is remarkable that it has survived through a period of intense competition with commercial providers of broadband internet and it is testament to the unique and local service it offers people in east Manchester. With Symera Technologies on board we can now move the service into a new and exciting phase."
This new joint venture allows Eastserve to further contribute towards Manchester’s ambition of being a world-class digital city and will help to ensure that all residents, through access to affordable next generation broadband, can benefit from the opportunities being created by the digital world.

He added: "East Manchester residents will now remain some of the best connected in the country."
For more information and to apply for the Eastserve internet service call 0161 231 9580, click on www.eastserve.com/broadband or call into the Eastserve office on Ashton Old Road in Beswick.
